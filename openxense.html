One product for AI chat and developer APIs. I built the gateway, the billing engine and the SaaS experience, and I operate its infrastructure.

The problem

Paying every AI provider separately adds up. People want chat; developers want an API. Both want one bill.

Why our own gateway

We could have exposed LiteLLM directly. A Go gateway in front of it keeps authentication, keys, quotas and traffic tracking in code we own.

Prepaid billing

Quota is denominated in USD, checkout is in IDR with QR-code payments, and margins are enforced through LiteLLM's max_budget per user.
